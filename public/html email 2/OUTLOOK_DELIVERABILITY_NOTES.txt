OUTLOOK AND DELIVERABILITY NOTES

This package is designed to reduce avoidable spam-filter triggers, but no email can be guaranteed to reach every inbox.

Before sending:
1. Send from your normal Metro Associates business mailbox.
2. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC are configured for metroassoc.com.
3. Use the recipient's real first name and company when possible.
4. Send to current, permission-based or legitimate business contacts.
5. Avoid large blasts from a new mailbox or domain.
6. Do not add URL shorteners, excessive links, attachments or tracking pixels.
7. Keep the included plain-text alternative.
8. Send a small internal test to Outlook, Gmail and a mobile device.
9. Use a real reply-to mailbox that is monitored.
10. For marketing campaigns, include the sender's physical mailing address and an appropriate unsubscribe method.

The .eml file embeds the hero image. The HTML file references the included PNG by filename.
